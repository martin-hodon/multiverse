# Greek paintings: vases and walls

Vase and wall painting in the Greek manner (2026-10-30): armed fights and processions drawn from parameters, in
black-figure, red-figure, white-ground, polychrome and painted relief -- `greek.js`, `greek/*.js`, data in
`assets/greek/canon.json` (`tools/greekgen.py`). Unlike the Egyptian murals (`docs/murals.md`), a figure is not a pose
from a table: bodies are solved, overlap, and aim at each other. Bench: `greek_lab.html`.

## What makes it Greek, and where each thing lives

| Layer | File | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| Rig | `greek/rig.js` | joints from an ACTION (legs, lean, twist, hands, each a range) and a scene's targets, by two-bone IK |
| Figure | `greek/figure.js` | PIECES from a pose: contours from asymmetric width profiles, head, helmet, shield, what the hands hold |
| Clothes | `greek/drape.js` | cloth derived from the pose: folds run along the same steps as its outline, by the period's style; a dress's `span` caps how wide its hem opens, so a striding rear leg comes out of it |
| Other bodies | `greek/beast.js` | a horse or ox by its gait, a car, furniture, near legs over the body and far under, a rider in its band of depth |
| Scene | `greek/scene.js` | who and what, independently; variants of what happens; prop links and hand reaches between bodies |
| Faults | `greek/faults.js` | what a painter wouldn't let stand in a solved group, pure reads, counted for the solver's redraws |
| Measure | `greek/measure.js` | a solved group's outline: `silhouette`, `unlike` (either way round), `notch` (the dip between two bodies) |
| Field | `greek/field.js` | a painting as a stack of zones: bands, solid, frieze, panels; a run of scenes picked for even gaps |
| Technique | `greek/paint.js` | the same pieces in any technique, a table of colours and line weights at the head of the file (`TECHNIQUES`; everything else is in `canon.json`) |
| Place | `greek.js` | pattern layer `{ kind: "greek", painting, seed }`, the viewer's sheet |

**A body faces either way, and flipping is not mirroring.** It is solved facing forward and placed; flipped, the near arm
changes (facing right the near arm is the right). So a right-facing hoplite shows his shield's inside with his far arm
through it, a left-facing one its blazon. `turn` is signed: chest to us when the far shoulder is forward, back when the near.

**The stroke-then-fill step** is the one drawing rule: a piece's rings are stroked, then filled, so only the outer half of the
line stays and only where it overlaps something. Black-figure incision is that stroke cutting the layer
(`destination-out`, so the clay shows through); red-figure's relief line is the same stroke in black. The ground goes in last,
behind everything, so red-figure's black background needs nothing else. The rings of one piece fill as one: a limb is thigh,
calf and foot with no line across its joints, and the trunk and near leg are one outline parted only by the groin's line.

**Periods** are multipliers (head, thigh, waist, chest) and choices (frontal or profile eye; zigzag or waved hem;
parallel or tension folds; fillers in gaps or none).

## Adding things

Everything below is data in `tools/greekgen.py`; no scene or who plays in it is named in the engine (`grep -E "hoplite|athena|duel"
greek/*.js` finds nothing; what can be worn is, so a cast member's `satyr` or `aegis` and a role's `seat`, its stool, are drawn by `figure.js` and `drape.js`), so these need no code:

- an **action**: legs program or `{ "of": [...] }` of them (`_ATTACK`, `_HOLD`, `_DEFEND`: one set of arms on many legs, so an attacker doesn't always lunge; panel redraws compare the legs drawn), `lean`, `turn`, `front`, each hand `[forward, up]` from its shoulder in arm lengths; ranges pick by seed. `arms` may be `{ "of": [...] }`, several ways of holding them on one trunk and legs (`_FREE` throws the empty hand; a body with a shield keeps the first, since the others put the shield in faces). Ranges alone barely move a body: a different set does.
- a **legs program**: feet `[forward, up, pitch]` in leg lengths, `pelvis` when the legs alone do not decide it.
- a **cast** member: `{ "p": chance, "v": value }`, `{ "of": [...] }` anywhere in it. `one`: comes once to a zone's panels (a god), by the panel redraw. `hose` gives zigzag leggings in the colours of the clothes. `skin` names a role for the body (`skinD`, darker, where a technique has it: giants and centaurs, so they part from those they fight; a technique without it falls back to `skin`).
- a **scene**: `roles` (cast, action, face, `at` from the first, `reach` a hand to another's anchor: `hair`, `head`, `throat`, `chest`, `belly`, `thigh`, `hand`, or a beast's; whoever is reached for is listed first), `links` (aim a prop at an
  anchor), `variants` (what happens, picked as a whole), or `chain` (a line of bodies, `actionFor` by who, `lead` what only the first one does). A role may be
  `p` (a chance of being there), `extra` (left out where a zone says `sparse`, a metope's two bodies; such a zone may tighten groups by `tight`, those with beasts by `tightWide`; though not around someone lying down, who needs his length. The bodies decide the size: shafts are held within the panel at the size it draws them, `fitGroup` in `greek/field.js`), `on` another's anchor
  (`onAt`, `onDx`), `aim` (a shaft carried at an angle), `tags` (for a beast's parts: `noHead`, `yoked`, `fewLegs`), `seat` (sits on a stool), `props` (what it holds, over the cast's). A scene may say `wide` (bodies too wide to tangle closer outside a panel), `scale` (the whole group drawn smaller, beasts and all, where a figure's height is fixed; in a panel the fit undoes it) and `reins` (`[rider, beast]`, a strap from hand to mouth).
- a **beat**: someone else who may be there, `{ cast, action, slots: [rooms it likes] }`; a scene gives `slots` (rooms: `at`, `face`) and `beats` (`n`, `of`). Left out where a zone says `sparse`.
- a **beast**: `anchors` (`seat`, `front`, `mouth`) and `parts` `{ z, role, rings, lines, if, unless }` by tag; a tag `dark` paints its body in the plain colour and a part's `roleIf` recolours it by tag (a black-figure lion's added-red mane); with `gaits`, a body and four legs too, its `foot` (a hoof, a paw: points off the leg's end), `role` (its colour) and `size` (to a man; a role may override it, a yoked team keeping its car's) in the data. A sphinx is a lion with tags `noHead` and `wings`: its head, chest and wing are the lion's own parts, by tag. Furniture, monuments and the car are parts alone (`_still`, `_car` in the generator).
- a **blow that lands**: `smite` brings a short weapon down on someone hurt (`_BEATEN`: `wounded` or `reel`, his weapon gone); a raised `strike` is only about to. A variant with someone lying inside a pool never survives `even` (it draws small, and the redraw picks another), so a decided fight is its own list entry (`gods-down`, with every `one` cast in it, or a late panel finds its gods spent). A side of metopes needs more than one way a fight goes: on the tile the attacker also loses (`centaur-down`, a horse on the `buckle` gait; `amazon-wins`; `troy-slain`, a man with nothing to hold up).
- a **wound**: a link whose shaft reaches someone whose action is `hurt` has gone in: the shaft ends on the body with no head showing, and `canon.drops` of blood run from it (role `blood`). At anyone else, or out of reach, the point stops `canon.shortOf` short, so it doesn't sit on him. A scene's `wounds` (`[who, anchor]`) bleeds where no shaft is, a lion's mouth.
- a **fight that holds**: face to face both lead legs point at each other and fuse, which `tangle` refuses, so `grapple` also has the victim turned away (`dragged`). A `seize` with `reach` to `hair` (a hand that can't get there walks its body in, same pose) (`grapple`, `amazon-seized`), or the taller body reaching down (`centaur-gripped`: a reach up to a centaur's head brings the near forearm across the reacher's own face, which `faults` counts). A scene on a `flow` frieze carries no one facing against it (`chariot-rite` is `chariot` without its fighter).
- a scene with **others in its roles**: `_as(scene, new, cast, *ids)` in the generator (the north metopes' Trojans). Retries favour a draw with a shield (points land on it, so fewer faults), so a low `p` on a cast's shield doesn't keep shields rare: give the role a cast without one. A cap takes the colour after the clothes, so a capped cast's `tint` stops short of the last (gold reads as a helmet). On a tile, leave out someone lying: he draws his panel smaller than `even` can hide.
- a prop's `at` fixes the hand whatever the action, so one held as a staff needs a copy without it to be thrust (`thyrsus-spear`), or its shaft crosses the holder's face.
- a **prop**: a `shaft` (the point lands on what it is aimed at, the shaft sliding through the fist), or `rings` from the grip;
  `at` where it is held, whoever holds it; `short` to aim only when near.
- a **blazon**, a **helmet**, a **headgear**, an **ornament** cell (`every` interrupts a band with another, the meander's saltire).
- a **painting**: `tech`, `period`, `aspect`, `ground`, zones `{ h, band | solid | scenes, panels, top, width, single, fill, tight, sparse, even, flow }` (`flow`: 1 or -1, a frieze's groups all one way, not by coin). A narrow field takes `single`: a frieze's run keeps whichever group fits best, and in a narrow field that is the same one every seed. Panels of one zone draw in shuffled order and redraw (up to twelve times) rather than do what another panel does or stand their bodies the same way (and a cast marked `one` isn't drawn again: `not` in `composeGroup` leaves it out of the variants and `of` lists, since `solveGroup`'s retries would otherwise land back on whichever draw has no faults), and with `even` a panel far under the middle one draws again, then none is larger than that many times the smallest, so a run holds one scale. A zone's `floor` (a share of the middle panel) stops one panel of someone lying from shrinking the rest with it.

Seeds run in streams by label (`stream(seed, "actor", id)`): changing the gear leaves the pose as it was.

## Where they are used

- **Pots**: `items/tableware/{amphora,krater,lekythos,hydria,kylix}` (`tools/propgen.py`, shapes in `tools/potshapes.py`), a lathe with `wrap: [W, H]` (`items.js`):
  one tile round the body and along its whole profile, lip to foot, four paintings by the item's seed. Set out at the Acropolis altar (`assets/architecture/acropolis.json`) and in the Greek house.
- **Parthenon**: metopes (a tile of eight, each side its subject, `FIGHTS` in the generator, a pool longer than the tile: panels deal from it shuffled by seed, so no scene comes twice and tiles differ; `width` .57 of a bay stays clear of the triglyphs, .84 m on a bay of 2.1 m, spaced from corner to corner of the frieze (`triglyphs.corner` in `arch/assemble.js`, so no half metope ends a side; `W` in the finish is eight of those bays); its joints behind them: the tile starts on a triglyph's line because the
  temple stands at the origin of its site) and the cella frieze; **Athena Nike** and the **Erechtheion**: friezes in `relief`, white on
  Eleusinian dark; **Pinakotheke**: a painted band in the gallery, a different picture on each wall (`GALLERY` in `tools/greekgen.py`), high enough to clear the door and window heads. Its rules name a wall by `facing` and fit a picture to it with `whole` (`arch/finish.js`), so none folds round a corner. Rules use `align: "above"`, the tile's foot on the
  band's own line, which a used finish moves with the building.

## Known limits

- A group retries (further apart, then another draw) and keeps the try with the fewest faults: a shield over a head or someone down, a near forearm or held cup or own shaft across a face, a near fist on his own head, one's feet planted inside another's (`tangle`), a shaft from an arm lost behind its body, two shafts lying as one, a head, chest, belly or shield under a horse's near body or legs not his own, a spear running on to another's head (a shaft carried at an angle is at no one), a held cup in another's face, one fallen with his hips or chest inside another's stride, two points meeting in the air, a point run through a shield (a link aimed past a shield in the way lands on the shield instead): `faults(data, actors, why)` in `greek/scene.js` (`why`, an array, collects `what:action` to tally causes over seeds); bodies tangling otherwise are only kept apart by each scene's `at`, so someone fallen goes where a gap is (behind the fight, before the horses): in black silhouette he would merge with the legs over him.
- Long paintings stay on a square layer of the tile array by running over several (`across` on a pattern, `archmat.js finishTexel`): 2048 wide over 4 layers on a 9.16 m wall is 224 pixels a metre, against 56 on one. Each layer costs 1.33 MB; the Acropolis's painted finishes take 26 or so.
- Tiles on the Parthenon are repeated: 8 metopes a side, not 92 different ones.

A pot's painting is fitted to its shape: `tools/potshapes.py` says its zones in heights on the pot, and `fit` turns them into shares of the painting, so a picture sits where the body is near its full width and isn't pinched at the feet. Its `aspect` is the picture's round over the profile's length; `wrap: [-W, 1]` covers the tile exactly once, right way up, the seam under a handle. A band's `squeeze` (its radius over the picture's) gives a narrow neck fewer cells; a panel's `at` places it (.25 is a one-sided pot's front, +z). A band may be `{ of: [names] }`, picked by seed, so ornament differs from pot to pot. A zone's `deal` gives a pot's few paintings each their own scenes by seed, not by a draw that may repeat; a scene's `rigid` keeps its spacing however tight the panel (a team yoked to its car). The inside of a pot is a separate lathe of glaze, except a kylix's: `disc: R` maps a unit tile from above, and a painting's `round: { r, w, band }` bends its ornament into a ring round the tondo.

A role's `on` may name a man as well as a beast (`onAt` an anchor on him, `onDx`, `onDy`; drawn over him: Anchises on Aeneas), and `size` draws a figure smaller than a man (an old man carried, a child). Cast `white` paints hair and beard in added white on every technique. A zone's `must` lists scenes that always come when its `scenes` list is longer than its panels, so a tile can draw from a wider pool and still keep its subject (north: `sack`, `priam`, `aeneas`).

A weapon held in an action marked `attack` aims at the nearest foe it faces (not of its own cast) unless the scene `links` it, and the body steps in to reach, by at most `canon.closeBy` and never nearer than `canon.closeMin`. In the generator, `fight(tag, A, B)` expands `_MOVES` (clash, cut, blow, over, chase) both ways between two lists of casts into scenes, so a side's pool comes from a few lines; a role's `wear` overrides what its cast has on (`shield: None`). `charge(tag, cast)` does the same for a man on four legs (`_CHARGES`). A charge's `w` puts someone between the two (a third body in a move's panel shrank it to half its neighbours'). Fault `shields`: two shields stacked; shield bearers stop `canon.closeShields` further apart. Implied aim is at the belly, so a blade slopes rather than lying along a chest. A charge may run the other way (`face`: the `kick` gait lashing back). Faults `own` (a point behind one's own shield) and `maw` (a head in a beast's head); nobody steps in across someone lying between. Implied aim needs a target armed, down or hurt. A role's `clutch` puts a hand on someone else after all are solved (`wristL`/`wristR` anchors: a victim holds the wrist that has him). A scene role's `props` may pick (`{of: [...]}`), as a cast's can.  A prop's `bulk` is the size of something held overhead: fault `cup` if it lands on a head; fault `blade` is a short weapon over another's face or lying on its own shield. A sparse zone solves a pair's span for its panel (`press`, the overflow a frame takes), redraws on a fault rather than widening, and weaves the two by `canon.weave`; a raised short weapon counts as a shaft for fitting (the bodies size a panel) and swings down rather than through the top. Giants are ordinary men in skin: dark read as racial coding, olive as orcs, sand as clay. A zone's `plan` deals a tile by arrangement: a scene's `shape` (`fight` tags each by its move) takes turns after `must`, no two neighbours alike, so tiles differ in their mix and not just their order. A painting's finish rule says `surface: wall`: figures go on walls, never on the underside of a beam or a narrow soffit, where a rule by height alone would also wrap them.

## Reading before filling

A pair in a metope is pressed toward a square, and a scene's `press` says how far: 1.1 by the zone, more for one that wants red between its bodies (`clash`, `chase`, `gods-beast`). The extra span is paid in size, so `press` is a distance, while the zone's value alone is how far past the sides a hand may go. If the pressed draw has faults, `fitGroup` steps `tight` back up: a smaller group that reads beats a larger one that doesn't. Faults for this: `hidden` (a shield over all of another's trunk), `fused` (one waist on another). A horse's `stout` thickens its legs under a centaur's trunk (tag `noHead`); a centaur's `size` is set in `greekgen.py`. A moves table entry in `_MOVES` says who carries a shield with `wear`, so not every fight is two shields meeting.

Variety across a tile is judged from what the panel looks like, not what its parts are called. `choose` (field.js) redraws a panel to raise its `unlike` from the panels already there (it stops at `canon.distinct`), adding `notch` (`canon.notched`) and taking off `canon.faulted` a fault. A group redrawn for its frame is the same draw: `solveGroup` gives `G.key`, and passing it back as `key` skips the search. A hand may reach a panel's side and no further, and a raised blade swings inward if its tip would pass it. A shield-bearer whose action leaves the left hand behind him holds it forward instead (`canon.shieldHold`, below `canon.shieldAhead`). Where a zone has a `plan` longer than its panels, the scenes left over stand in once a panel's own has had four tries at looking different.
